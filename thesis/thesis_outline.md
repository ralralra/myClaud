# 과학문명과 인간의 철학적 이해
## Scientific Civilization and Human Philosophical Understanding

**Subtitle**: Why We Built AI, Why We Fear It, and What It Means to Remain Human

---

## Thesis Statement

Humanity built science out of an intrinsic drive to overcome perceived insufficiency — but in creating artificial intelligence, we have produced a mirror that reflects our own thinking back at us, exposing both our cognitive limits and our irreplaceable humanity. The real question of the AI age is not whether machines can surpass us, but whether humans will continue to think, judge, and take responsibility for themselves.
